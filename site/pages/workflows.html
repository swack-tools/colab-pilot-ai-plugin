The 6-phase zero-waste GPU protocol

  1. 0-CU local gate: Validate archives, manifests, and Python syntax locally before renting any VM.
  2. 0-CU Drive pre-staging: Stage inputs larger than 24 MiB to Google Drive via colab_smart_transfer (mode="drive_stage") with zero GPU burn.
  3. BF16 GPU cascade: Call colab_allocate_cascade to try A100-highmem → A100 → G4 → L4 and probe VRAM, System RAM, and bf16_supported.
  4. Headless Drive mount: Mount Drive read-only on the VM via colab_mount_rclone with streaming or random-seek VFS presets.
  5. Detached execution & REST polling: Launch training with colab_launch_detached and monitor progress with colab_poll_job over REST.
  6. Artifact pull & verified teardown: Download outputs, call colab_stop_all, and confirm Active assignments: 0 (0.00/hr).

Autonomous colab-operator subagent

In Antigravity and compatible subagent hosts, delegate end-to-end training or multi-shard extraction to colab-operator. The agent follows rules/AGENTS.md automatically, negotiates GPU cascades, recovers from CUDA out-of-memory errors with colab_reset_vram, and guarantees zero orphaned VMs before returning.

Hardware & OOM safety rules

  • Check bf16_supported first: Do not enable torch.bfloat16 on GPUs such as T4 that lack native BF16 support.
  • Set DataLoader(num_workers=0) when caching in RAM: Avoids Linux fork() copy-on-write System RAM OOM-killer crashes on Colab VMs.
  • Flush zombie VRAM before retrying: Call colab_reset_vram after any CUDA OOM or exception to clear sys.last_traceback and IPython Out references.
  • Guard short-run EMA decay: Warm up exponential moving average decay with min(0.9995, (1 + step) / (10 + step)).

Detached watchdog & escalating wakeup alarm

Long runs use colab_launch_detached (which wraps nohup, normalizes \r tqdm progress lines, and writes a 10-second GPU heartbeat JSON) paired with scripts/colab_watchdog.py. When a job finishes, stalls, or fails, colab_wakeup_alarm triggers a macOS multi-voice speech and accelerating chirp alert until acknowledged.
